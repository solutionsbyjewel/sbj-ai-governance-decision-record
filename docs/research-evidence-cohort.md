# SBJ AI Governance Research Evidence Cohort

**External evidence provenance for enterprise AI governance research across agentic AI, Physical AI, AI assurance, and AI security maturity.**

**Publisher:** Solutions by Jewel (SBJ)  
**Publication:** SBJ AI Governance Research Evidence Cohort  
**Research program:** SBJ AI Governance Architecture V2.0  
**Status:** Published research evidence record  
**Canonical publication:** https://solutionsbyjewel.com/ai-governance/research/research-evidence-cohort/

## Purpose

The SBJ AI Governance Research Evidence Cohort documents external evidence retained during Solutions by Jewel's V2.0 research and connects that evidence to the governance questions and research domains it helped pressure.

The cohort supports research provenance, attribution, evidence classification, source-quality controls, research boundaries, and traceability.

## Evidence domains

The publication organizes retained evidence across four domains:

- **Agentic AI and enterprise authorization** — AI agent governance, identity, delegated authority, permissions, action traceability, stale authorization, recovery, and evidence sufficiency.
- **Physical AI and industrial execution** — perception provenance, transformation, effective context, actuation, safe-state distinctions, execution identity, and reconstruction.
- **External assurance and disposition** — evidence production, independent assessment and validation, continuing evidence, contextual reliance, product-specific authorization, and shared responsibility.
- **AI security maturity** — operational AI security maturity, governance, controls, and evidence expectations.

## Evidence sources

The retained evidence record includes primary organizational and technical documentation, government sources, published research, operator/vendor case evidence, and practitioner evidence from live events.

Organizations and systems represented in the publication include NIST, Anthropic, Microsoft, SANS Institute, GovRAMP, Waymo, Boston Dynamics, Meta / Project Aria, Apollo Tyres and AWS, MSP Corp, MS Solutions, Cyderes, and published research sources.

Named practitioners and public-source contributors are identified in the canonical publication where the retained evidence supports attribution.

## Research use

The evidence cohort supports continuing SBJ research in:

- AI agent governance and agentic AI governance
- AI agent authorization and delegated authority

## Source-quality controls

SBJ research gives preference to primary organizational, government, authored technical, and published research sources appropriate to the question being evaluated.

Evidence classifications preserve the source type and the scope of what each source establishes. Technical capability, connectivity, execution, and logging are evaluated within their documented evidentiary scope.

## Attribution boundary

Solutions by Jewel independently evaluated publicly documented and directly observed evidence for V2.0 research.

Inclusion in the Research Evidence Cohort identifies an external evidence source. It does not imply participation in, sponsorship of, endorsement of, certification of, approval of, or validation of SBJ methodology or architecture.

The **Research Evidence Cohort is not a V2.0 benchmark cohort**.
